Section 8

Attestation

Read the following terms. You will be asked to confirm that you have read them, which you will not have.

Article I — Nature of this form

This form is an application to submit a grievance. It is not a grievance. Submission of this form does not create a grievance, indicate that a grievance exists, or oblige any person to believe that one might.

Article II — Receipt

Receipt of this form is acknowledged by the issuing of a reference number. The reference number acknowledges only the receipt of the reference number.

Article III — Response

The Office endeavours to respond to all applications within a reasonable period. The Office determines what is reasonable. The Office has determined that it is longer than this.

Article IV — Amendment

Sections of this form may not be revisited once completed. Errors discovered after completion may be corrected by beginning a new form, at which point the error will be preserved for comparison.

Article V — Escalation

You may escalate a grievance to the Review Panel. The Review Panel reviews the conduct of the Office in receiving grievances, not the grievance. The Review Panel last convened in 2017 and reported that receipt had occurred.

Article VI — Withdrawal

You may withdraw your application at any time by completing Form DV‑1140‑W, which is available on request. Requests are processed by the Office of Grievance Intake, in the order received.

Article VII — Feelings

The Office does not accept grievances submitted on behalf of a feeling. Where a grievance is found to be substantially a feeling, it will be returned with the feeling removed.

Article VIII — Silence

Where the Office does not respond, the matter is deemed resolved to the satisfaction of all parties. Where the Office does respond, the matter is deemed to be continuing.

Article IX — This article

This article is included so that Article VIII is not the last thing you read. You have now reached the end of the terms. This fact has been recorded.

Discrepancies between signatures are recorded permanently.
